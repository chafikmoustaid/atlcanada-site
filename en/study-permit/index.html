Study permit in Canada: application, extension, change

Whether you are applying from abroad or already studying in Canada: the steps, the documents to prepare, what to know to extend your permit or change schools, and the points where a case is won or lost.

The steps, from admission to departure

  1. Choose a program at a designated learning institution: a study permit is only issued to study at one.
  2. Get your letter of acceptance and, when required, the provincial or territorial attestation letter (PAL/TAL).
  3. Gather proof of funds: first-year tuition, living costs and transportation costs.
  4. File the application online with IRCC, then give your biometrics (from Morocco: at a VFS Global centre).
  5. Respond to IRCC's requests until a decision is made, then prepare your arrival.

Points to prepare carefully

Attestation letter (PAL/TAL)

Required for most new applications, with exceptions set by IRCC. For 2026, one exception covers master's and doctoral students at a designated public institution.

Proof of funds

You must prove your first-year tuition, living costs and transportation costs. For applications filed from September 1, 2026, IRCC requires at least $23,448 CAD in living costs for a single person, excluding tuition and transportation (outside Quebec: Quebec sets its own requirements with the CAQ). IRCC reviews this amount regularly: we confirm it with you at the consultation.

Biometrics

Fingerprints and a photo are given by appointment; from Morocco, at a VFS Global centre. For a temporary resident, they stay valid for 10 years.

Letter of acceptance

It must come from a designated learning institution and match the program named in your application.

To study in Quebec, a provincial step is added (the CAQ): we go through it with you at the consultation if your plan goes that way.

Already in Canada?

Extending your study permit

To stay at the same institution, file the application at least 30 days before the permit expires. If you stay in Canada, you can keep studying under the same conditions until a decision is made. The permit cannot be extended beyond your passport's expiry date.

Changing institution

Apply for the extension before finishing your current program. In general, you cannot start at the new school before the extended permit is approved, with some exceptions.

Studies longer than planned

File the application at least 30 days before the permit's original expiry date.

Studies finished early

The permit expires on the earlier of two dates: the date on the permit, or 90 days after the end of your studies (from the school's first notice or the issue of your degree).

The school loses its designated status

You can keep studying until the permit expires or move to another designated institution. To extend, you must be enrolled at a designated institution.

Is your study permit expired, or are you unsure? Talk to us before acting: the rules depend on your situation.

After your studies

Depending on the program and the institution, a post-graduation work permit may be possible. That Canadian work experience can then count toward a permanent residence application, for example through Express Entry.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a study permit?

Generally yes (this is the case for Moroccan citizens) when the program lasts more than six months. For a shorter course the rules differ: we check your case with you.

Which documents do I need to provide?

The exact list is set by IRCC according to your situation. It usually includes the passport, the letter of acceptance, proof of funds and, when required, the PAL/TAL. We validate it with you before filing.

How long does processing take?

Timelines change regularly: IRCC publishes them on its website and we promise no timeline. We tell you the current situation at your appointment.

When should I apply to extend my study permit?

At least 30 days before it expires, according to IRCC. If you stay in Canada, you can keep studying under the same conditions until a decision is made.

Do I have to use a consultant?

No, you can file your application yourself. A regulated consultant helps you choose the right strategy, prepare a complete file and avoid omissions.

Do you guarantee that I will get the permit?

No. No serious consultant can guarantee a decision: it belongs solely to the Canadian authorities.
